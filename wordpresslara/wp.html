01 / FEATURED WORK ACADEMIC ACTIVITY

IoT Activities

Throughout our IoT activities, we had the opportunity to explore and experience how technology works through hands-on learning. We tried different activities, such as making an LED blink and using a seven-segment display, which helped us understand the basics of electronics, programming, and connecting hardware components. At first, some tasks were challenging, but as we worked on them, we learned how each component functions and how small programs can control electronic devices. These activities were not only about following instructions but also about trying things ourselves, solving problems, and learning from our mistakes. Overall, our IoT experience helped us gain new knowledge, improve our technical skills, and become more familiar with how the Internet of Things is used in real-life applications.

05 / FEATURED CASE STUDY

Our Capstone Project.

Technology developed with purpose, collaboration, and a commitment to creating practical solutions.

OPOL COMMUNITY COLLEGE

NSTP Integrated Management System

The NSTP Integrated Management System for Opol Community College is a web and mobile-based solution developed to digitize and organize NSTP Office operations.

It addresses manual recordkeeping and administrative processes by providing a centralized platform for student information, documents, activities, attendance, training hours, and certificates.

LARAVEL REACT NATIVE EXPO PHP MYSQL JAVASCRIPT

System Highlights

  • Student Record Management
  • Document Submission and Tracking
  • OCR-Assisted Document Processing
  • Attendance Monitoring
  • Training Hours and Eligibility Monitoring
  • Activity and Schedule Management
  • Certificate Generation
  • Announcements and Notifications
